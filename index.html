Quick fixes

Try these before anything else.

🐢 My PC is slow
  1. Press Ctrl+Shift+Esc to open Task Manager and see what uses 100% of CPU, memory or disk.
  2. Disable useless startup apps (Startup tab).
  3. Keep at least 15% of your disk free.
  4. If you still use an old hard drive (HDD), an SSD is usually the biggest upgrade.
🖥️ No display on the screen
  1. Plug the screen cable into the graphics card, not the motherboard (if you have a graphics card).
  2. Try another cable or another screen.
  3. Switch off, unplug, and re-seat the RAM sticks firmly.
  4. Listen for beeps at startup: they give a hint about the problem.
🔥 My PC is hot or noisy
  1. Switch off and unplug, then blow the dust out with compressed air (fans, filters, heatsinks).
  2. Check that all fans spin.
  3. Leave space around the case for air to flow.
  4. If the PC is several years old, the thermal paste may need replacing.

Not solved? Describe the problem to the assistant above.
